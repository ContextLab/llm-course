Key Concepts

α (Alpha)

Document-topic concentration. Lower α means documents focus on fewer topics. Higher α means documents spread across many topics.

β (Beta)

Topic-word concentration. Lower β means topics have distinct vocabulary. Higher β means topics share more words.

θ (Theta)

Per-document topic distribution. Each document has its own θ defining how much it belongs to each topic.

φ (Phi)

Per-topic word distribution. Each topic has its own φ defining probabilities over the vocabulary.

Generative Process

1

For each topic k

Draw word distribution φk ~ Dirichlet(β)

2

For each document d

Draw topic distribution θd ~ Dirichlet(α)

3

For each word n in document d

Draw topic zd,n ~ Multinomial(θd)

Draw word wd,n ~ Multinomial(φz)

Inference: Gibbs Sampling

Since we observe only words (shaded node), we need to infer the hidden topics. This demo uses Collapsed Gibbs Sampling:

Topic Assignment Update:

P(zi = k | z-i, w) ∝ (nd,k-i + α) × (nk,w-i + β) / (nk-i + Vβ)

Probability of assigning word i to topic k depends on: how much topic k appears in this document, and how often this word appears in topic k.
