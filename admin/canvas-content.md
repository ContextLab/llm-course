## Course Description

**PSYC 51.07: Models of Language and Communication**

This course introduces students to the various approaches used in building, studying, and using conversational agents and large language models. Through hands-on programming exercises, readings, and discussions, we explore historical approaches (ELIZA, PARRY), modern transformer architectures (BERT, GPT), and emerging techniques (RAG, Mixture of Experts).

**Key Topics:**
- Pattern matching and rule-based chatbots
- Text preprocessing, tokenization, and embeddings
- Attention mechanisms and transformer architecture
- Encoder models (BERT) and decoder models (GPT)
- Retrieval-augmented generation and advanced techniques
- Ethical considerations in AI development
